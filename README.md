## Zepto Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes sales performance of beauty and personal care products on Zepto, covering revenue, customers, quantity sold, order status, payment methods, top products and top brands.

## Zepto Dataset
- <a href="https://github.com/Arizdli/Zepto_sales_dashboard/blob/main/Zepto%20data.csv">Zepto data</a>

## Dataset Questions
- What is the total revenue, total quantity sold and total number of customers?
- What is the average revenue per customer?
- What is the average price of products?
- How does revenue change month by month?
- Which products have the highest and lowest average price?
- Which products sell the highest quantity?
- Which are the top 10 brands by revenue?
- What percentage of orders are delivered and what percentage are cancelled?
- How much revenue is lost to cancelled orders?
- Is the cancellation rate higher for COD than for UPI or Credit Card?
- How many customers placed repeat orders?

## Tools and Skills
-Power BI (DAX measures, slicers, interactive visuals)
- Data cleaning and modeling
- Sales and retail analytics, dashboard storytelling
